Three Money Systems — Distribution Model

Step through one year at a time and compare how value accumulates with the Bank, Government and Community.

How Money Actually Works

This model uses the monetary framework set out by Stuart Kells in Fragile Prosperity. The propositions below are treated as the operating assumptions of the simulation.

The conventional story Kells rejects

  • Money is treated as an official, durable unit that circulates through the economy.
  • Governments are assumed to collect taxes, hold that money, and then use it to fund spending.
  • Government deficits are assumed to inject new money equal to the gap between spending and taxation.
  • Banks are assumed to lend out deposits received from savers.
  • The banking system is described through a loan multiplier or credit multiplier.
  • A fraction of deposits is assumed to be held as reserves, giving rise to the conventional fractional-reserve story.
  • Central banks are assumed to control the money supply by changing the quantity of reserves.
  • Inflation is treated as having many possible causes, with official interest rates used as the principal tool for suppressing demand.

Monetary rules used as truth in this model

  • Bank money, created as contractual ledger-based IOUs, is the dominant form of money in the economy.
  • Banks create deposits when they make loans; they do not first obtain deposits and then lend those deposits out.
  • Bank lending therefore creates new money.
  • Bank reserves are used mainly for settlement within the banking system and do not operate as a simple quantitative constraint on lending.
  • Central banks do not directly control the quantity of bank money by controlling reserves.
  • Taxation at the national level removes liquidity from the economy, mainly by extinguishing ledger-based bank-money balances.
  • Where bank-created money is directed matters for inflation, asset prices, productivity and wealth distribution.
  • In Australia, residential-property lending has been a major source of bank-created money, so property prices are not independent of the credit-creation process.
  • Financial regulation has concentrated on prudential safety while giving insufficient attention to the economy-wide balance between productive and unproductive lending.

What the Three Money Systems model tests

The simulation holds the initial act of money creation constant and changes what happens afterwards. It measures how much value accumulates with the Bank, Government and Community under three different rule sets.

The central question is: after new money has created an asset, who ultimately receives the value generated by the financing and by the asset?
